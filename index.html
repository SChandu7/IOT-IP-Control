ESP REMOTE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026 · Published by InfuTechz

Short version: ESP Remote does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Everything you set up stays on your phone, and the app only talks to the device address you type in yourself.

What the app does

ESP Remote turns your phone into a controller for an ESP32 or any other device that answers HTTP requests on your local network. You choose the device address, the button names and the endpoints each button calls.

Information we collect

None. We do not run a server, we do not have a database, and we never receive anything from your phone.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your own settings locally so they are still there next time you open it:

WhatWhyWhere
Device address (for example 192.168.4.1)So the app knows where to send commandsOn your phone only
Control layouts, button names and endpointsTo restore your HUDOn your phone only
Appearance and timing settingsTo keep your preferencesOn your phone only

Deleting the app removes all of it. You can also clear it from your system settings under app storage.

Network use

The app sends HTTP requests only to the address you enter. Those requests normally go to hardware on your own Wi-Fi network and never leave it. No data is sent to us or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Internet / network access — needed to reach your device over Wi-Fi. This is the only permission the app requests.
  • Vibration — optional button feedback, and you can switch it off in settings.

Third parties

No advertising networks, no analytics or crash reporting services, no social logins, and no third-party trackers of any kind.

Children

The app is a hardware control tool and is not directed at children. Since no data is collected, nothing is collected from children either.

Safety note

ESP Remote controls real motors and hardware. You are responsible for operating your device safely, in a suitable area, and for the safety cut-off logic on your own hardware. The app is provided as is, with no warranty.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
